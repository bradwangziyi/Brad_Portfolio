About

Hi, I'm Brad!

I’m a Mechatronics Engineer who likes taking a project the whole way: designing it in CAD, choosing how to manufacture it, wiring up the electronics, and writing the code that runs it. I care most about practical designs that solve real problems and hold up outside the lab. My interests include embedded systems, CAD, advanced manufacturing, and sustainable engineering.

Currently studying at McMaster University and always open to opportunities for collaboration and learning.
